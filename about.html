About

I'm Mahe Roggero, a Creative Director working across visual identity, art direction, and digital experiences. My work sits at the intersection of strategy and craft — building brands that are both meaningful and unmistakably distinctive.

Growing up between France, the UK, and years spent across Europe and the Americas has shaped the way I think about communication — I move fluently between cultures, languages, and audiences, which gives my work a perspective that's both grounded and adaptable.

Over the years I've worked across fashion, culture, sport, and hospitality — collaborating with brands that demand rigour and a genuine point of view. I care about the full arc of a project, from the first concept to the final pixel.

I'm currently open to new opportunities worldwide — whether that's an in-house role, agency collaboration, or freelance project. If you're looking for a creative director who brings both strategic thinking and hands-on craft, let's talk.
